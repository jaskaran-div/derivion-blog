MACRO LIQUIDITY

THE GREAT INDIAN LIQUIDITY SEESAW:

From Surplus to Squeeze

BY MONETARY POLICY RESEARCH GROUP

In autumn 2026, Indian banking liquidity plunged 99% - from a surplus of ₹11 lakh crore in mid-Sept to just ₹541 crore by late Oct. Driven by $133B in FCNR(B) inflows followed by ₹1.5–2 lakh crore in GST tax drains, Jio IPO lockups, and festive cash withdrawals. The RBI responded with ₹1.5 lakh crore in VRR injections to defend the 5.50% policy corridor.

Mega IPO Mechanics

BY CAPITAL MARKETS DESK
