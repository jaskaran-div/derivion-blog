THE NEW ERA OF INDIAN K–12:

Education Investment & Brand Partnerships

CPI Tops Expectations as Gasoline Surge Drives Headline Print

Global markets saw sharp volatility after the latest U.S. Consumer Price Index release. A steep 9% monthly surge in gasoline accounted for more than half of the headline increase, while overall energy prices jumped 5.2% on the month - up sharply from 2.1% in August.
