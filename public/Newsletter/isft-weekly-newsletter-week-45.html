BEHAVIORAL FINANCE · BLOG 14

WHY YOUR BRAIN SABOTAGES YOUR TRADING PLAN

BY BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS DESK

Financial volatility triggers a limbic hijack, shifting cognitive control from analytical System 2 thinking to emotional System 1 survival instincts. Under Prospect Theory, traders experience the pain of a loss twice as intensely as equivalent gains—prompting early exits on winning positions and dangerous holds on losing ones. Mitigate this through automated order pre-commitments and mandatory 30-minute cooling-off periods.

MARKET STRUCTURE · BLOG 13

THE END OF FREE TRADING?

Broker Business Models

BY CAPITAL MARKETS GROUP

Discount brokerages face an acute financial squeeze as regulators enforce "True-to-Label" mandates, eliminating lucrative exchange rebate slabs that previously subsidized zero-fee equity delivery. Coupled with stricter F&O margin rules and higher contract lot sizes, major retail trading platforms are rapidly pivoting toward Margin Trading Facilities (MTF), mutual fund distribution, and premium research tiers.

Macro & Treasury Refunding Update · Capital Markets

TREASURIES RALLY AS MIDTERM OUTCOMES RESHAPE FISCAL EXPECTATIONS

DERIVION ANALYSIS

The US Treasury's updated refunding guidance maintained a bond-friendly tone by signaling potential reductions in long-end bond issuance. Combined with sharply lower energy prices and steady October ISM Services expansion, sovereign yields fell across global benchmarks. However, Wall Street institutions warn that upcoming structural fiscal gridlock sets the stage for a major debt ceiling showdown in 2027.

Derivion Executive Takeaway: With energy price spikes unwinding and central banks approaching terminal rate thresholds, market focus shifts to execution discipline. Institutional desks are prioritizing systematic risk controls while retail platforms navigate structural fee recalibrations.

COGNITIVE BIASES IN TRADING (BLOG 14)

Confirmation Bias: Filtering chart patterns to validate open trades while ignoring risk signals.

Anchoring: Fixating on execution purchase price rather than evolving market structure.

Loss Aversion: Feeling 2× psychological pain from losses, causing traders to run unmanaged risk.
